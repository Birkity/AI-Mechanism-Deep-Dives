# Evening Call Summary

- Date: Day 4, Week 12
- Participants: Birkity Yishak and Amir Ahmedin

The main feedback was that the reliability answer should not choose between raw agreement and kappa as if one number could settle the question. What landed was the combined reporting pattern: observed agreement for practical stability, kappa for chance correction, base rates for prevalence context, and AC1 or PABAK as a companion metric when prevalence is extreme. What still needs follow-up is qualitative review of `workflow_correctness` disagreement cases, because the statistics flag the weak dimension but cannot prove whether it is rubric ambiguity or inherent semantic difficulty. The revision made the protocol-name correction explicit: same rater, 24-hour gap means intra-rater test-retest reliability, not true inter-rater agreement.
