# AI Mechanism Deep Dives - Week 12

This repo contains my Week 12 paired knowledge-gap research artifacts: daily questions, morning-call sharpening notes, explainers, public threads, source lists, signoffs, and grounding updates back into Week 10/11 portfolio work.

Day 4 is the final day of this Week 12 compounding sprint.

## Structure

- `pair_DAY_1/` through `pair_DAY_4/` - daily paired deliverables
- `synthesis.md` - week-level synthesis across all four days
- `canonical_list.md` - annotated reading and pattern list
- `portfolio_update.md` - Week 10/11 grounding updates
- `ENV_SETUP.md` - environment notes

## Daily Folder Contents

- `question.md`
- `morning_call_summary.md`
- `explainer.md`
- `thread.md`
- `evening_call_summary.md`
- `signoff.md`
- `grounding_commit.md`
- `sources.md`

## How To Use

1. Each morning, fill `question.md` and `morning_call_summary.md`.
2. During the day, draft `explainer.md` and `thread.md`.
3. After the evening call, update `evening_call_summary.md`, `signoff.md`, and `grounding_commit.md`.
4. Add `sources.md` with canonical sources and tool or pattern references.
5. At week end, complete `synthesis.md`, `canonical_list.md`, and `portfolio_update.md`.

## Day Snapshots

- Day 1: instruction/rubric drift in long judge loops; cautious vs overconfident language at inference time.
- Day 2: reasoning-chain causality in judge verdicts; prompt constraints vs schema-constrained decoding.
- Day 3: verdict signal vs explanation-pattern mimicry; near-miss rejections for grounded SDR outreach.
- Day 4: conditional accuracy under abstention; raw agreement, kappa, and prevalence-aware reliability reporting.
